# ASTRAWILD Echoes Database Specification

## Overview
Echoes are primordial spirits manifested through harmonic resonances in Astrawild. Each Echo possesses unique elemental affinities, combat abilities, and work proficiencies.

---

## 1. Pyrelite (The Ember Fawn)
- **Stable ID**: `Echo.Pyrelite`
- **Species Name**: Pyrelite
- **Title**: The Ember Fawn
- **Affinity**: Solar / Flare
- **Weakness**: Torrent / Hydration
- **Base Attributes**:
  - Max Health: 280
  - Attack Power: 42
  - Defense: 22
  - Movement Speed: 620 cm/s
- **Abilities**:
  - `FlameDart`: Launches a rapid thermal dart dealing 35 Solar damage + 3s Burn.
  - `SolarSurge`: Increases movement speed by 30% and emits a radiant heat pulse for 6s.
- **Work Suitabilities**:
  - Kindling / Smelting: Level 1
  - Foraging: Level 1
- **Capture Baseline Difficulty**: Moderate (Modifier 1.0)

---

## 2. Aquavine (The Dew Serpent)
- **Stable ID**: `Echo.Aquavine`
- **Species Name**: Aquavine
- **Title**: The Dew Serpent
- **Affinity**: Torrent / Hydration
- **Weakness**: Geo / Verdurous
- **Base Attributes**:
  - Max Health: 340
  - Attack Power: 36
  - Defense: 28
  - Movement Speed: 500 cm/s
- **Abilities**:
  - `BubbleCascade`: Emits a barrage of pressurized water spheres dealing 28 Torrent damage and slowing target by 25%.
  - `DewCleansing`: Emits a rejuvenating mist healing companion/player for 15 HP/s over 4s.
- **Work Suitabilities**:
  - Irrigation / Watering: Level 1
  - Gathering: Level 1
- **Capture Baseline Difficulty**: Moderate (Modifier 0.9)
